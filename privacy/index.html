LittleTank

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

At a glance

LittleTank has no app accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or app-operated servers. The aquarium app does not send your fish names, tank designs, or settings to the developer.

Your aquarium

Aquarium designs, names, decorations, and settings are saved locally in shared app storage, called an App Group, so LittleTank and its widget use the same aquarium. Recovery copies and wallpaper crops saved by earlier versions may also remain in local storage. LittleTank does not upload these files.

Artwork cache

LittleTank keeps reusable fish and decoration graphics in a local cache to help widgets appear faster. These files contain app artwork, not activity logs or advertising identifiers. iOS may remove cached files when it needs space.

Apple services and backups

iOS, the App Store, and device backup features are operated by Apple. Whether app data is included in device or cloud backups depends on your device and backup settings. LittleTank does not operate a separate cloud sync or backup service.

Reset and removal

Reset aquarium replaces the aquarium shown in the app with a starter aquarium. It does not securely erase every saved file: recovery copies, older wallpaper images, and cached graphics may remain. You can request removal of locally stored app data by using Delete App in iOS; Offload App retains app documents and data. iOS controls this removal. Existing backups may retain earlier app data and must be managed separately in your device or backup settings.

Websites and support

The online privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening those pages or submitting a support issue is handled by GitHub under its own privacy practices. GitHub support may require a GitHub account. Information you choose to include in a public support issue is visible to others; it is not sent automatically by LittleTank.

For help or questions about this policy, visit LittleTank support or contact through a GitHub issue.

Policy updates

This policy may change when LittleTank’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
