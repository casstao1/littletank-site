A little help.

Setup and troubleshooting for LittleTank: Fish Tank Widget.

Add your widget

Touch and hold your iPhone or iPad Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, and find LittleTank. Add the Aquarium widget in small, medium, or large.

Customize your aquarium

Open LittleTank. The fish button manages your fish; the aquarium button edits the tank. Save tank changes with the checkmark.

Fish movement

In the LittleTank app, fish swim continuously. On the Home Screen, the shipping widget shows still fish poses and refreshes them on iOS's timeline schedule.

If the widget needs a refresh

Open LittleTank after saving your changes. iOS controls when Home Screen widgets refresh, so an update may take a moment to appear. If a widget from an older development version is blank, remove that widget and add Aquarium again.

Background appearance

Your selected water color is shared with the widget. System glass follows iOS's native widget appearance. Wallpaper visibility depends on the Clear Home Screen appearance on supported iOS versions. LittleTank leaves your Home Screen icons and appearance choices unchanged.

Ask for help or report a problem

Open a support issue with your app version, iOS version, widget size, and the steps that led to the problem.

Contact LittleTank support ↗
