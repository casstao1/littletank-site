A little help.

Setup and troubleshooting for LittleTank: Fish Tank Widget.

Add your widget

Touch and hold your iPhone or iPad Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, and find LittleTank. Add the Aquarium widget in small, medium, or large.

Customize your aquarium

Open LittleTank. The fish button manages your fish; the aquarium button edits the tank. Save tank changes with the checkmark.

Fish movement

Fish swim continuously in the LittleTank app. The Home Screen widget also animates your fish, with motion managed by iOS. Animation can slow down or pause in Low Power Mode, under other power-saving conditions, or after an iOS update.

If the widget needs a refresh

Open LittleTank after saving your changes. iOS controls when Home Screen widgets update, so changes may take a moment to appear. If the widget is blank or stops moving, open LittleTank first, then try removing the widget and adding Aquarium again. Turning off Low Power Mode can also help restore movement.

Background appearance

Your selected water color is shared with the widget. System glass mode can let your wallpaper show through with a soft water effect. Transparency and softness depend on your iOS version, Home Screen appearance, and how iOS renders the widget. LittleTank does not change your other Home Screen icons or appearance settings.

Ask for help or report a problem

Open a support issue with your app version, iOS version, widget size, and the steps that led to the problem.
